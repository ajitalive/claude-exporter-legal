Privacy Policy

Chat & Project Exporter for Claude.ai · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This extension is made by an independent developer ("the developer", "we", "us"). It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic. "Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.

The short version

  • Your chats, projects and files are read from your own Claude.ai account, in your browser, and stay on your computer.
  • We do not run servers that receive your chats, files, search index, folders, bookmarks or notes.
  • We have no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and we never sell or share your data.
  • The only information that leaves your computer for us or our payment provider is a license key check (details below).

What the extension does with your data

When you use the extension while signed in to claude.ai, it requests your projects, chats, files and usage figures from claude.ai using your existing login, the same way the Claude website does. It processes that content inside your browser to let you export, search, organise and back it up. It does not copy that content to any server of ours.

What is stored on your computer

The extension stores the following in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), on your device only:

  • A search index of your chat text, so you can search messages.
  • Your folders, bookmarks, notes and tags.
  • Settings (for example save folder, backup schedule) and the web addresses you chose to sync.
  • Your license key and activation details, and a daily download counter for the free plan.
  • The status of your last backup.

Exports and backups you create are saved as ordinary files in your Downloads folder, in the sub-folder you choose. Removing the extension deletes the stored data above. Files already downloaded remain on your computer until you delete them.

What is sent to others

  • Freemius (license checks). When you activate a Pro license and about once a week afterwards, the extension sends your license key, a random identifier generated for this browser, and a device label (such as "Chrome extension (Win32)") to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, to confirm the license is valid. Payments are handled entirely by Freemius, which acts as the seller of record. See Freemius's own privacy policy for how it handles purchase data.
  • Websites you ask it to read. If you use "Sync knowledge from", the extension fetches the web page, GitHub repository, or folder you specify, directly from your browser. The site you choose will see your request, as with any visit.
  • claude.ai. Actions you trigger (such as cloning a project, copying files, syncing, or deleting files) are sent to claude.ai through your own logged-in session.

We do not receive your chat content, files, or Claude.ai account information.

Permissions

The extension asks for browser permissions only to provide its features: reading your Claude.ai pages and account data, saving files to Downloads, storing its data locally, scheduling backups, showing usage notifications and a right-click menu for bookmarks, and (only when you ask) reaching websites you choose to sync. Each is used solely for these functions.

Your choices

  • You can delete all extension data at any time by removing the extension.
  • "Deactivate this browser" removes your license from this browser and frees the activation.
  • You can export your folders, bookmarks and notes to a file, and import them back.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the extension's store listing.
